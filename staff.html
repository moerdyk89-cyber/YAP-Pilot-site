Common questions

Frequently asked

How do I get my tips?

Your tips are made available quickly after your shift. When you're ready, message YAP on WhatsApp to request a withdrawal, and it's paid straight to the bank account your employer has registered for you.

When will my withdrawal arrive?

Once you request a withdrawal, PayShap payments usually reflect in your account within minutes. If it's been longer than that, message YAP on WhatsApp and reply MISSING — it'll be investigated.

Can I get airtime, data, or a voucher instead?

Right now, tips can be withdrawn to your registered bank account. Savings facilities, airtime, data, and other voucher options are coming soon to the same WhatsApp chat.

How do I check what's available?

Message YAP on WhatsApp and reply BALANCE to see what's currently available to withdraw.

Can I get a payslip?

Yes — reply PAYSLIP and choose the month you need. It shows your basic salary and tip income as separate line items, both SARS-compliant, and works as proof of income for loans or rental applications. An IRP5 is issued annually.

What happens if my tip amount looks wrong?

Tip amounts are calculated from what's recorded at your venue for your shift, split according to your employer's rules. If something looks off, reply DISPUTE with the date and the amount you expected — YAP will check the records and come back to you within one business day.

What does YAP do with my personal information?

YAP processes your information only to make your tips available, pay out withdrawals, and send you notifications. Your details aren't sold or shared with anyone other than the payment providers that process the transfer. YAP complies with POPIA — you can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time.

Is YAP a bank? Is my money safe?

YAP is operated by Vectory FS (Pty) Ltd, part of the Paymenow group. YAP doesn't hold your money in a bank account of its own — withdrawals are sent directly to your bank account via PayShap, operated by the South African Reserve Bank and BankservAfrica.

How do I stop getting WhatsApp notifications?

Reply STOP and YAP will stop sending tip notifications — your tips will still be made available as normal. Reply START anytime to turn notifications back on.

Something's wrong — how do I get help?

Message YAP on WhatsApp and reply HELP at any time. You'll reach the main menu, or someone from the team will follow up with you directly.

Still have a question? Message YAP on WhatsApp and reply HELP any time, or speak to your venue manager directly.
