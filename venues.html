For venues

Zero cash handling. Zero end-of-shift disputes.

Why we're running a pilot

Real POS data, real shifts, real staff.

We're running a 2–3 month pilot with a small group of Johannesburg restaurants and hospitality venues to test tip-split logic across different shift structures and see how staff take to the WhatsApp interface — before a wider rollout.

What we ask of you

Five things, none of them heavy lifting.

  • Connect YAP to your existing POS system — we handle the technical side
  • Provide daily POS tip data extractions for the duration of the pilot
  • Brief your staff on the wallet — we provide simple onboarding materials
  • Allow us to collect anonymised transaction data during the pilot
  • Share feedback at two check-in points (mid-pilot and end-of-pilot)
  • Nominate one internal point of contact for day-to-day queries
Service commitments

What YAP commits to during the pilot.

The full SLA covers the pilot period (4–8 weeks) and runs alongside the Pilot T&Cs. Here are the targets that matter day to day.

WhatTarget
Venue sends daily tip dataBy 10am the next morning, for the prior day's trade
YAP runs reconWithin 3 working hours of receiving the data
YAP pays withdrawal requestsSame day recon is done — 2 batches, at 1pm and 5pm
Payment issue flagged and fixedWithin 2 business days
YAP support responseWithin 1 business day, WhatsApp or email
Deduction schedule sent to venueWeekly or monthly, depending on your cycle

Fees: the only charge is on withdrawals — a flat R10 per transaction, minimum withdrawal R200. No cost to the venue during the pilot beyond reimbursing tips disbursed.
