How it works

From a tip at the table to money in your pocket.

The pilot runs as a manually-operated programme — YAP handles recon, payment files, and staff notifications by hand, so the model can be proven before the tech is fully automated. For staff, the experience is identical either way: they get their tips, whenever they want, at a minimal fee.

Daily flow

Five steps, once a day.

01

Venue shares end-of-day tip data

Each day, the venue exports tip totals from the POS and shares the file with YAP — a simple daily send via email.

02

YAP runs the recon

The data is reconciled, the venue's agreed split rules are applied, and each staff member's share is calculated.

03

YAP processes payments via PayShap

Tip amounts are floated and sent as individual payments to each staff member — the amount they request, same day. No cash handling on the venue's side. Minimum withdrawal R200, flat fee of R10 per withdrawal.

04

Staff receive a WhatsApp notification

Staff engage entirely through WhatsApp — seeing what's available, requesting withdrawals, and getting support. No app download needed.

05

Venue reimburses YAP

At the end of each cycle (weekly or monthly), YAP invoices the venue for the total tips disbursed via a consolidated deduction schedule. The venue reimburses in a single payment.
