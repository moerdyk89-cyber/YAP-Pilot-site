The daily flow

From table to bank account in four steps.

01

Venue exports the day's tips

A simple daily send from the POS — email or WhatsApp, whichever's agreed.

02

YAP runs recon

Tip totals are checked against your agreed split rules and calculated per staff member.

03

Tips are made available, fast

Each staff member's share is ready in their YAP wallet in no time, with a WhatsApp message to match.

04

Staff request a withdrawal

Whenever they're ready, it's paid straight to their bank account via PayShap.
